Privacy Notice

FeildHaus Founding Teams Pilot

FeildHaus is currently operating as a limited pilot for recreational sports teams. We collect only information needed to run team workspaces and improve the pilot.

What we collect

Team and captain account information, roster information entered by captains, game-day responses and assignments, device/session information needed for sign-in and notifications, and feedback submitted through the pilot.

How we use it

We use this information to provide team features, keep team workspaces separate, support game-day tools, send requested reminders, troubleshoot issues, and improve FeildHaus.

Team privacy

Each team has its own workspace. Captain-only information is not intentionally exposed in the player view, and pilot feedback is stored privately with the team workspace.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Service providers used to host and operate the app may process data as necessary to provide the service.

Retention and deletion

During the pilot, information may be retained while a team is active or while needed for support, security, and troubleshooting. Captains may request workspace deletion or removal of pilot data.

Pilot status

FeildHaus is an independent pilot and is not an official Stonewall Sports product or endorsement unless explicitly stated otherwise.
