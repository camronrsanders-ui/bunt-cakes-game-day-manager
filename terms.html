Pilot Terms

Founding Teams Pilot

FeildHaus is being provided as an early pilot for invited recreational sports teams. By using the pilot, you agree to use it responsibly and only for lawful team activities.

Beta service

The pilot may change, contain bugs, or experience downtime. Features and data formats may change as we learn from real game-day use.

Captain responsibility

Captains are responsible for the roster, schedules, links, assignments, and other team information they enter or publish to their players.

Rules and officiating content

FeildHaus may organize league rules and training references for convenience. The controlling league rulebook and authorized league officials remain the final source for official rulings.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse accounts, access another team's workspace without authorization, interfere with the service, or upload content you do not have permission to use.

No official league affiliation

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, FeildHaus is independent and is not endorsed by, sponsored by, or an official product of Stonewall Sports or any other league.

Feedback

Pilot feedback may be used to improve FeildHaus, prioritize features, and evaluate whether the service should expand.
