CAPTAIN QUICK START

Get your Haus game-day ready.

You do not need to configure everything at once. Complete these five steps, share Player View, and use FeildHaus during a real game.

Create or open your Haus
1

Create your Haus

Use the Founding Team invite code you received. Create your Captain account, then FeildHaus opens your private team workspace.
2

Set up the team

In Team Settings add the team name, colors/logo, location, time zone and game-day reminder timing.
3

Add roster + schedule

Add the people who actually play, then add at least one upcoming game. RSVP and attendance use this roster.
4

Build game day

Use Fielding to assign positions, Kicking for order, and Officiating for umpire/line-ref responsibilities. Captains control these plans; Player View stays simple.
5

Connect the players

Open Player Access, generate each player's one-time setup link, and send it directly to that player. After pairing, their device stays connected to the correct roster identity.

Before your first game

  • Roster reflects who is actually on the team.
  • Upcoming game date/time/location are correct.
  • Players have answered availability or attendance is updated.
  • Fielding and kicking plans are ready.
  • Officiating assignments are filled when required.
  • At least one player has opened Player View on their phone.

During the pilot

Use the Pilot Feedback button whenever something breaks, feels confusing, saves you time, or gives you an idea. Short notes are enough. FeildHaus records the team and screen so the feedback is easier to investigate.

What players need to know

Player View is for the information they need on game day: availability, schedule, field assignment, kicking order, officiating responsibilities and team resources. They should not need Captain access.
